\begin{abstract}
We give an effective proof of the asymptotic conjecture stated by Akwei for
a specified noncyclic pair-moment relaxation of the Sidon problem.
The construction is Akwei's renewal boundary profile and capped Fej\'er
covariance, with a positive-part repair and explicit finite error bounds.
For every integer \(N\ge2^{60}\), its optimum lies between
\(\sqrt N+(2\sqrt2/3)N^{1/4}-2572\) and
\(\sqrt N+(2\sqrt2/3)N^{1/4}+1\).
The lower proof averages the complete renewal cancellation inequality across
the centered endpoint atoms, retaining the sign of the renewal jump.
It separates a triangular \(O(N^{-1/4})\) error from a global
\(O(N^{-1/2})\) error. The upper bound transfers Chen's finite-measure
energy estimate to the relaxation. No numerical feasibility campaign is used.
\end{abstract}

\section{The exact relaxation and attribution}
For \(N\ge2\), let \(\Htwo(N)\) maximize
\(\kappa=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1} f_j\) over real \(f\) and symmetric \(\Gamma\), subject to
\begin{gather}
 \begin{pmatrix}1&f^{\mathsf T}\\f&\Gamma\end{pmatrix}\succeq0,\qquad
 \Gamma_{jj}=f_j,\quad 0\le f_j\le1,\quad \Gamma_{ij}\ge0, \label{model}\\
 \sum_{j=0}^{N-1-d}\Gamma_{j,j+d}\le1\qquad(1\le d<N). \label{budgets}
\end{gather}
This is exactly Akwei's Definition 7.2 \cite{akwei}. The feasible set is
compact: the diagonal is bounded and PSD gives
\(|\Gamma_{ij}|^2\le f_i f_j\le1\).
A Sidon indicator gives a feasible pair, but a general feasible pair need not
be realized by a set or a probability distribution on sets.

\begin{theorem}\label{main}
For every integer \(N\ge2^{60}\),
\[
 \sqrt N+\frac{2\sqrt2}{3}N^{1/4}-2572
 \le\Htwo(N)<
 \sqrt N+\frac{2\sqrt2}{3}N^{1/4}+1.
\]
The lower bound has the explicit construction given below.
\end{theorem}

The conjecture, coefficient, renewal profile, centered atoms, covariance
ansatz and cap are due to Akwei \cite[Section 7]{akwei}. His preceding
Theorem 7.6 is computational at four values of \(N\), and Conjecture 7.7
states the all-\(N\) bounded-remainder assertion. The contribution here is
the uniform finite-interval analysis, including transition lags and
constant objective loss. The upper companion is an attributed consequence
of Chen's positive-measure bound \cite{chen} and the PSD trace argument
already used by Akwei. It does not improve Chen's physical Sidon bound.
Hou--Zhao \cite{hz} provide the original finite smoothing framework.
Constants and onset in this note are deliberately conservative.

\section{Scope, reproducibility and review status}
The theorem concerns only \eqref{model}--\eqref{budgets}. It does not exclude
stronger pair models containing further valid quadratic or distinct-sum
constraints. Covariance PSD is not a realizability theorem
\cite{kuna}. No claim about a Sidon-set lower construction, a coefficient
below the smoothing floor, or a resolution of Erd\H{o}s problem 30 follows.

The accompanying evidence packet contains the frozen statement,
three complete proof sections, two fresh independent internal mathematical
reviews with source hashes, objection resolutions, and a separate bounded
primary-source comparison. No optimizer, FFT, sampled feasibility,
renewal table or numerical campaign is used. Existing rational smoothing
certificates and public releases are unchanged.
